Fix Website

Choose the website's pictures in Odoo

The people who look after the website pick its pictures here instead of in code: device photos or drawings, the original logo of every brand, and photos for every repair service. The website reads them from the booking catalogue it already calls.

Website Details: drawings or photos

One toggle shows device types (Mobile Phones, Laptops, Printers…) as drawings or as their chosen photo, across the whole site. Both stay saved. "Load starter pictures" fills empty places only.

Device Pictures

One switch at the top, "Show real photos on the website": off, every device type shows its drawing; on, its real photo. Drawings come in 5 styles (3D, Line, Flat, Sketch, Duotone) and "Drawing style" picks one style for the whole site, so all device tiles match. Only one look is used at a time and both stay saved. Below it, every device type with its drawing and photo; "Edit pictures" opens its drawing and photo gallery, where "Show on website" picks which photo is used.

Switch to Real Photos: the drawing controls disappear and every device type shows its photos; tap one to use it (green border and ✓). Every choice is saved straight away.

Inside a device type: its photos side by side, the one used marked "On website".

Brand Logos

The original logo of every brand, phones to CCTV. "Missing logo" shows what is left to add.

Service Pictures

Every repair service with its shown photo; filters for "Missing picture" and "Fewer than 3 photos".

Inside a service: its photos side by side. The one on the website carries a green badge; "Show on website" switches it, and "Add Website photos" uploads your own.

The editor: the page drawn as the website draws it

Home Page, Services Page and Info Pages open in the editor: the page on the left, drawn with the website's own colours, fonts and layout; click any part of it (header, a section, the footer) and its wording and lines open in the panel on the right. Changes show at once and are saved as you type ("Saved" chip). Drag a section to reorder it, press the eye to hide it, switch between Desktop and Phone.

A section opened: every field says where it shows, with a hint and an example. Hover a field (or its eye) and the part of the page it changes lights up on the left. The page redraws while you type.

The panel's title bar stays on top while you scroll: "All sections" takes you back, the "Editing" list jumps to any other part, and the arrows step through them in page order.

"What we fix": show or hide each device tile with the eye; the Most booked repairs can be reordered, taken off, or added with a search.

Phone view, and the footer with the contact details, quick links and branches.

The Services page: heading, the repair flow, the coverage areas (zones with their areas as chips; type an area and press Enter) and the FAQ.

Brand Logos and Service Pictures use the same editor: the brands strip and every brand card as the site draws them; click a brand to upload or replace its logo, tick it into the strip, set its caption. Filters find brands with a missing logo.

Service Pictures: every repair service as a card with its website photo; click one to add photos (several at once), pick the one shown, or tick it into "Most booked".

Info pages: the list with each page's footer column, and one page as it opens on the site.

Services page wording (the plain form)

Website Details ▸ Services page: the heading, the repair-flow titles, the fee label, the coverage title and the trust points under the areas ({price} becomes the price shown). The repair flow itself (the technician comes first; fixed on the spot, or to our lab and back) is edited once, in Home Page ▸ How we fix it, and shows on both pages.

Coverage Areas

The zones the site says it covers, each with its areas in order. The website shows the Doorstep zones; Pickup zones are kept but not shown. A new area finds its map position on OpenStreetMap when saved, so the "check my location" box on the site recognises it.

FAQs

The questions at the bottom of the Services page and on the FAQ page. Drag to reorder, switch one off to hide it.

Info Pages

Pages such as About us, Privacy policy and Terms at /page/<web address>, each listed in a footer column (Company, Help, Policies). Contact, Areas and FAQ pages fill themselves; a text page shows once it has text.
