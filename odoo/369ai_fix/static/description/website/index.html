Fix Website

Choose the website's pictures in Odoo

The people who look after the website pick its pictures here instead of in code: device photos or drawings, the original logo of every brand, and photos for every repair service. The website reads them from the booking catalogue it already calls.

Website Details: drawings or photos

One toggle shows device types (Mobile Phones, Laptops, Printers…) as drawings or as their chosen photo, across the whole site. Both stay saved. "Load starter pictures" fills empty places only.

Device Pictures

One switch at the top, "Show real photos on the website": off, every device type shows its drawing; on, its real photo. Drawings come in 5 styles (3D, Line, Flat, Sketch, Duotone) and "Drawing style" picks one style for the whole site, so all device tiles match. Only one look is used at a time and both stay saved. Below it, every device type with its drawing and photo; "Edit pictures" opens its drawing and photo gallery, where "Show on website" picks which photo is used.

Switch to Real Photos: the drawing controls disappear and every device type shows its photos; tap one to use it (green border and ✓). Every choice is saved straight away.

Inside a device type: its photos side by side, the one used marked "On website".

Brand Logos

The original logo of every brand, phones to CCTV. "Missing logo" shows what is left to add.

Service Pictures

Every repair service with its shown photo; filters for "Missing picture" and "Fewer than 3 photos".

Inside a service: its photos side by side. The one on the website carries a green badge; "Show on website" switches it, and "Add Website photos" uploads your own.
